Philosophy

Do you know the feeling of DJ..DJ..your sound is always the same? Suddenly pulled into a circle, passionately awakening in a remarkable way — the feeling of being transported through music. Jesse feels this deeply, and transmits his sound and emotions to the crowd with radical sensitivity.

Grown up in the infectious party spirit of legendary clubs like Aera, Labyrinth, and SpiderGalaxy, Jesse learned the importance of equal weight at the entry, the main act, and the outro. Every track, every transition, every moment matters.

Galaxy Space Night has represented electronic music on community radio for over 15 years. Every Saturday on Radio RaBe, every other Thursday from Zürich at the witching hour on Radio LoRa — presenting the latest from the electronic sound world.

A loyal vinyl lover who equally enjoys the CDJ. A DJ who measures every breath of the journey with precision and soul.
